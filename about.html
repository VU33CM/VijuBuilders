Who We Are

Experience That Shapes Every Structure.

Viju Builders is a construction and engineering firm established in 1990. With a foundation in civil engineering and building construction, the company focuses on creating practical, well-planned and lasting spaces.

Our work spans residential construction, commercial buildings, civil engineering works, renovation and extensions, along with architectural modelling and visualization.

We believe that every successful project begins with understanding the client's requirements and bringing together the right planning, technical knowledge and execution.
